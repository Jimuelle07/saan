# Tax deductions checklist (personal, filing year 2025)

Gather documents by the end of February. Decide standard vs itemized once totals are in.

## Income documents
- [ ] W-2 from employer
- [ ] 1099-INT from the high-yield savings account
- [ ] 1099-DIV and 1099-B from the brokerage
- [ ] 1099-NEC for freelance design work

## Possible itemized deductions (Schedule A)
- [ ] Mortgage interest - Form 1098 from the lender
- [ ] Property taxes paid (state and local taxes capped at the SALT limit)
- [ ] Charitable donations - receipts for anything over $250, mileage for volunteer driving
- [ ] Medical expenses above 7.5% of adjusted gross income

## Above-the-line adjustments
- [ ] Traditional IRA contribution (deadline is the filing date in April)
- [ ] HSA contributions made outside of payroll
- [ ] Student loan interest - Form 1098-E (up to $2,500)
- [ ] Educator expenses if applicable

## Self-employment (Schedule C)
- [ ] Home office: square footage of the dedicated room vs whole home
- [ ] Software subscriptions, a portion of internet and phone
- [ ] New laptop - consider Section 179 expensing
- [ ] Quarterly estimated tax payments made (dates and amounts)

## Credits
- [ ] Child and dependent care - daycare provider tax ID
- [ ] Energy efficient home improvement credit for the heat pump installation

Keep everything for at least 3 years; 7 years for anything involving bad debt or worthless securities.
