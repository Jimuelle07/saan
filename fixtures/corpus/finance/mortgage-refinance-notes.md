# Should we refinance the mortgage?

Current loan: 30-year fixed at 7.1%, balance $382,000, 27 years left. Payment (P&I) $2,570.

## Quotes received
| Lender            | Rate  | Points | Closing costs | New P&I |
|-------------------|-------|--------|---------------|---------|
| Credit union      | 6.125%| 0      | $4,900        | $2,321  |
| Online lender     | 5.875%| 1.0    | $8,200        | $2,260  |
| Current servicer  | 6.25% | 0      | $3,100 (streamline) | $2,352 |

## Break-even
- Credit union: saves $249/month -> 4,900 / 249 = about 20 months.
- Online lender: saves $310/month -> 8,200 / 310 = about 26 months.
- We plan to stay at least 7 more years, so both pay off.

## Other considerations
- Restarting a 30-year term adds 3 years of payments; could pay extra each month to keep
  the original payoff date.
- Appraisal needed for the credit union; our home value went up, so we'd drop PMI too.
- Rate lock for 45 days costs nothing at the credit union.

## Decision
Go with the credit union, no points. Ask about a 20-year term quote as well.
