Packing list - Iceland ring road, late September

Outer layers
  - Waterproof shell jacket and rain pants (rain comes sideways)
  - Insulated jacket, fleece mid layer
  - Merino wool base layers, top and bottom
  - Warm hat, buff, waterproof gloves

Footwear
  - Waterproof hiking boots, broken in
  - Microspikes for the glacier walk (or rent them on site)
  - Flip flops for the hot springs and pool locker rooms

Car / road
  - Phone mount and car charger
  - Offline maps downloaded; check road.is and the safetravel app every morning
  - Snacks - gas stations are far apart on the east side

Swim
  - Swimsuit (you must shower naked before entering public pools - it's the rule)
  - Quick-dry towel

Other
  - Sleep mask - it's still light until 8pm
  - Camera with extra batteries (cold drains them fast)
  - Tripod for northern lights photos
  - Reusable water bottle - tap water is excellent
