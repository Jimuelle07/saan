# Iceland ring road - 9 days clockwise

Rental: 4x4 SUV (gravel protection insurance - yes, take it).

| Day | Route                          | Drive  | Highlights                                    |
|-----|--------------------------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 1   | Keflavik -> Reykjavik          | 50 min | Hallgrimskirkja, harbor, hot dog stand        |
| 2   | Golden Circle -> Hella         | 3 h    | Thingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss                 |
| 3   | Hella -> Vik                   | 2 h    | Seljalandsfoss, Skogafoss, black sand beach   |
| 4   | Vik -> Hof                     | 3 h    | Fjadrargljufur canyon, Skaftafell glacier hike|
| 5   | Hof -> Hofn -> Djupivogur      | 3 h    | Jokulsarlon lagoon, Diamond Beach             |
| 6   | Djupivogur -> Egilsstadir      | 3 h    | East fjords, small fishing villages           |
| 7   | Egilsstadir -> Myvatn          | 2.5 h  | Dettifoss, Hverir geothermal area, nature baths |
| 8   | Myvatn -> Akureyri -> Borgarnes| 5 h    | Godafoss, whale watching from Akureyri        |
| 9   | Borgarnes -> Keflavik          | 2 h    | Blue Lagoon on the way to the airport         |

## Notes
- Never stop on the road for photos; use the marked pull-outs.
- Sneaker waves at Reynisfjara are deadly: stay far back from the water.
- F-roads (highland tracks) are not allowed with our rental contract.
- Fill up whenever the tank is half empty in the east.
- Aurora forecast: vedur.is cloud cover map, look for KP 3+ and clear skies.
