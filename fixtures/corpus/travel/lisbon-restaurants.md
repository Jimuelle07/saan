# Where to eat in Lisbon

Collected from friends and two visits. Prices are rough per person.

## Pastries
- **Pasteis de Belem** - the original custard tarts, warm, with cinnamon. Line moves fast. EUR 2
- **Manteigaria** (Chiado) - many locals say these are better. Fresh every few minutes.

## Seafood
- **Cervejaria Ramiro** - garlic prawns, clams Bulhao Pato, and finish with a steak sandwich
  (prego) for dessert as is tradition. No reservations; go at 6pm. EUR 40
- **Ponto Final** (across the river in Almada) - grilled fish on a pier at sunset. Take the ferry
  from Cais do Sodre. Book ahead. EUR 35

## Casual
- **Time Out Market** - food hall, good for groups who can't agree.
- **A Cevicheria** - Peruvian ceviche, giant octopus hanging from the ceiling.
- **O Trevo** - bifana (pork sandwich) and a beer standing at the counter. EUR 5

## Fado dinner
- **Tasca do Chico** in Bairro Alto - tiny, loud, authentic. Arrive before 8pm.

## Drinks
- Ginjinha (sour cherry liqueur) at A Ginjinha near Rossio, in a chocolate cup.
- Miradouro de Santa Catarina for sunset beers.

Tip: the bread, olives and cheese put on the table at the start are not free - send back what you don't want.
