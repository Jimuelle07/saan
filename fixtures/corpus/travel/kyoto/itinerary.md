# Kyoto trip - 5 days in November (autumn leaves)

Hotel: small ryokan near Gion, 10 minute walk to Kawaramachi station.

## Day 1 - Arrival
- Haruka express from Kansai airport to Kyoto Station (75 min). Buy ICOCA card.
- Evening walk through Gion and Hanamikoji street. Dinner: kaiseki at the ryokan.

## Day 2 - Eastern hills
- 6:30am Kiyomizu-dera before the crowds, then Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka lanes.
- Yasaka Shrine, Maruyama park.
- Afternoon: Philosopher's Path to Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion). Peak maple color here.
- Night illumination at Eikando temple (book tickets in advance).

## Day 3 - Arashiyama
- Early train to Saga-Arashiyama. Bamboo grove at 7am.
- Tenryu-ji garden, Okochi Sanso villa (matcha included with entry).
- Monkey park on Iwatayama - steep 20 minute hike.
- Sagano scenic train back along the river gorge.

## Day 4 - Fushimi Inari and Nara day trip
- Fushimi Inari: walk the torii gates to the Yotsutsuji intersection for the view.
- Afternoon in Nara: Todai-ji great Buddha, feed the deer (bow to them first).

## Day 5 - Markets and departure
- Nishiki market breakfast: tamagoyaki, pickles, soy milk donuts.
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) if time allows.
- Shinkansen to Tokyo at 3pm.

Budget: about 25,000 yen per day per person excluding the hotel.
