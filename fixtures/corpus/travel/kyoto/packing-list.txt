Packing list - Japan, November

Clothes
  - Layers: temperatures 8-17C. Light down jacket, two sweaters, long sleeve tees
  - Comfortable walking shoes that slip on and off easily (temples, ryokan - shoes come off a lot)
  - Socks without holes! You'll be in socks in front of people
  - One nicer outfit for the kaiseki dinner

Documents and money
  - Passport, printed hotel confirmations
  - Cash in yen - many small shops and shrines don't take cards
  - Coin purse (lots of 100 yen coins)
  - Travel insurance card

Electronics
  - Type A plug adapter (same as US, but no grounding pin - bring a 3 to 2 prong adapter)
  - Pocket wifi reservation pickup at the airport, or eSIM
  - Portable battery

Misc
  - Small towel / handkerchief (public restrooms often have no paper towels)
  - Small trash bag - very few public trash cans
  - Goshuin book for temple stamps
  - Pain reliever, blister plasters
