# Vegan chocolate chip cookies

Chewy middles, crisp edges, no eggs or butter. Makes about 18.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup (113 g) refined coconut oil, melted, or vegan butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup unsweetened oat milk
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 2.5 tbsp water (flax egg), rested 5 minutes
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup dairy-free dark chocolate chips
- Flaky sea salt for the top

## Steps
1. Whisk the oil and both sugars until glossy, about 1 minute.
2. Add oat milk, flax egg and vanilla; whisk again.
3. Fold in flour, baking soda and salt until just combined. Stir in chocolate.
4. **Chill the dough 30 minutes** - coconut oil dough spreads too much otherwise.
5. Scoop 2 tbsp balls onto a lined tray, 3 inches apart.
6. Bake at 350F / 175C for 10-11 minutes. Edges set, centers look slightly underdone.
7. Sprinkle flaky salt, cool on the tray 10 minutes.

Check that the chocolate chips are actually dairy-free; many semi-sweet brands contain milk fat.
