Napa cabbage kimchi (mak kimchi) - small batch, fills a 2 liter jar

INGREDIENTS
  1 large napa cabbage (about 1.5 kg)
  1/4 cup coarse sea salt
  Paste:
    1 tbsp sweet rice flour cooked with 1/2 cup water into a thin porridge, cooled
    1/4 cup gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes)
    2 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce to make it vegetarian)
    1 tbsp salted shrimp, minced (optional)
    6 cloves garlic, 1 inch ginger, 1/2 Asian pear - blended
    1 tsp sugar
  Vegetables: 4 scallions in 2 inch pieces, 1/2 cup julienned daikon, 1 carrot julienned

STEPS
  1. Cut cabbage into bite-size pieces. Toss with salt, let sit 2 hours, turning every 30 minutes.
  2. Rinse 3 times in cold water and drain well for 20 minutes.
  3. Mix the paste ingredients. Wear gloves!
  4. Toss cabbage and vegetables with the paste until everything is red and coated.
  5. Pack tightly into a clean jar, pressing out air. Leave 2 inches of headspace.
  6. Ferment at room temperature 1-2 days. Press down once a day; you'll see bubbles.
  7. Taste: when it's pleasantly sour, move it to the fridge. Keeps for months and gets more sour.

Old, very sour kimchi is perfect for kimchi fried rice or kimchi jjigae stew.
