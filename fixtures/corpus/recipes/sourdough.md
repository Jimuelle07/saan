# Country sourdough loaf

Makes one large boule. Total time about 24 hours, mostly waiting.

## Ingredients
- 100 g active starter (fed 4-6 hours earlier, bubbly and doubled)
- 375 g water, around 80F / 27C
- 450 g bread flour
- 50 g whole wheat flour
- 10 g salt

## Method
1. **Autolyse**: mix flour and 350 g of the water until no dry bits remain. Rest 1 hour.
2. Add the starter, squish it in with wet hands. Rest 30 minutes.
3. Add salt and the remaining 25 g water. Pinch and fold until absorbed.
4. **Bulk fermentation**: 4-5 hours at room temperature. Do 4 sets of stretch and folds,
   30 minutes apart, during the first 2 hours. The dough should grow by about 50% and jiggle.
5. Pre-shape into a round, rest 20 minutes uncovered, then final shape tightly.
6. Place seam-side up in a floured banneton (rice flour prevents sticking).
7. **Cold retard**: cover and refrigerate 12-16 hours overnight. This develops the sour flavor
   and makes scoring easier.
8. Preheat the oven with a Dutch oven inside to 500F / 260C for 45 minutes.
9. Turn the dough onto parchment, score with a razor at a shallow angle.
10. Bake covered 20 minutes, then lower to 450F / 230C and bake uncovered 20-25 minutes
    until deep brown. Internal temperature should be around 208F.
11. Cool at least 1 hour before slicing (it's still cooking inside).

## Troubleshooting
- Flat loaf: under-proofed or weak shaping. Check that the starter floats in water.
- Big holes under the crust: under-fermented bulk.
- Gummy crumb: sliced too early.
