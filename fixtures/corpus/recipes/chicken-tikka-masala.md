# Chicken tikka masala

Serves 4.

## Marinade
- 700 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp grated ginger, 4 cloves garlic, 1 tsp salt

Marinate at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.

## Sauce
- 3 tbsp ghee or butter
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi), crushed between palms
- Fresh cilantro to finish

## Steps
1. Thread chicken on skewers and broil on high 10-12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
2. Melt ghee, cook onion over medium heat until deep golden, about 12 minutes.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste and spices, stir 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add tomatoes, simmer 15 minutes until the oil separates at the edges.
5. Blend smooth if you like, then stir in cream and fenugreek.
6. Add the charred chicken, simmer 10 minutes.
7. Garnish with cilantro. Serve with basmati rice and naan.

Tip: the char from the broiler is what gives the smoky flavor, don't skip it.
