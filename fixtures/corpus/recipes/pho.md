# Beef pho (weekend version)

Serves 6. The broth takes 6 hours, but it's mostly hands-off.

## Broth
- 2.5 kg beef bones (knuckle and marrow), 500 g beef brisket
- 2 onions and a 4 inch piece of ginger, halved
- Spices: 5 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, 6 cloves, 1 tbsp coriander seeds, 1 black cardamom pod
- 3 tbsp fish sauce, 1 small piece of rock sugar, salt to taste

1. Parboil the bones 10 minutes, dump the water and scrub the bones. This keeps the broth clear.
2. Char onions and ginger directly over a gas flame or under the broiler until blackened.
3. Toast spices in a dry pan until fragrant, put them in a mesh bag.
4. Simmer bones, brisket, onion and ginger in 6 liters of water. Never let it boil hard.
5. Pull the brisket after 1.5 hours, chill it, then slice thinly.
6. Add the spice bag for the last hour only, otherwise it gets bitter.
7. Season with fish sauce, rock sugar and salt. Strain.

## Bowls
- Flat rice noodles, soaked then blanched 20 seconds
- Raw eye of round sliced paper thin (freeze 20 minutes first to make slicing easy)
- Ladle boiling broth over the raw beef to cook it in the bowl

## Toppings
Thai basil, bean sprouts, lime wedges, sliced jalapeno, cilantro, hoisin and sriracha on the side.
