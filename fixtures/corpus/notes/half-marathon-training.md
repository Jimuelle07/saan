# Half marathon training plan (12 weeks)

Goal race: Riverside Half, October 18. Target time: under 1:55 (about 8:45 per mile).

## Weekly structure
- Monday: rest or yoga
- Tuesday: intervals (e.g. 6 x 800m at 5k pace, 400m jog recovery)
- Wednesday: easy 4 miles
- Thursday: tempo run, 3-5 miles at comfortably hard effort
- Friday: rest
- Saturday: long run, building from 6 to 12 miles
- Sunday: easy recovery 3 miles or cross-training (bike)

## Long run progression
| Week | Miles |
|------|-------|
| 1    | 6     |
| 3    | 8     |
| 5    | 9     |
| 7    | 10    |
| 9    | 12    |
| 11   | 8 (taper) |

## Fueling
- Practice with energy gels on runs over 75 minutes: one gel every 40-45 minutes.
- Electrolyte drink on hot days. Don't try anything new on race day.

## Injury notes
- Left shin felt tight in week 4. Replaced shoes (old pair had 450+ miles).
- Foam roll calves and do eccentric heel drops three times a week.
