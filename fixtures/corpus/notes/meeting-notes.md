# Maple Grove Neighborhood Association - Monthly Meeting

Date: April 14, community center room B. Attendance: 23 residents, 5 board members.

## Agenda

1. Park renovation proposal
2. Street parking complaints
3. Summer block party
4. Treasurer's report

## Park renovation

- The city offered a matching grant of up to $40,000 for playground equipment.
- Residents voted 19 to 4 to replace the wooden play structure with a fenced, accessible one.
- Concern raised about losing the shade trees near the swings; the landscape architect will revise the plan.
- Volunteers needed for a cleanup day on May 3.

## Street parking

- Several complaints about cars blocking driveways on Elm Court.
- Board will ask the city for "no parking" signs near the fire hydrant.

## Block party

- Tentative date: July 19. Bounce house quote is $350.
- Mrs. Alvarez will coordinate the potluck sign-up sheet.

## Treasurer's report

- Dues collected: 84% of households. Balance: $6,212.
- Spending on the newsletter printing is down after moving to email.

Next meeting: May 12, 7pm.
