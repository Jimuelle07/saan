# Vegetable garden log 2026

## Bed layout
- Bed 1: tomatoes (Cherokee Purple, Sungold), basil between plants
- Bed 2: zucchini, bush beans
- Bed 3: lettuce, radishes, carrots (succession sowing every 2 weeks)

## Log

**Apr 20** - Hardened off the tomato seedlings for a week, then transplanted after the last frost date.
Buried the stems deep, about two thirds of the plant, so they root along the stem.

**May 6** - Added straw mulch to keep soil moisture even. Watering deeply twice a week instead of daily.

**May 28** - First signs of blossom end rot on two tomatoes. Likely uneven watering rather than lack of
calcium. Set up a drip line on a timer.

**Jun 15** - Pinched suckers off the indeterminate tomatoes. Staked with 7 foot cedar stakes.
Aphids on the beans - sprayed with diluted soap water, ladybugs showed up a few days later.

**Jul 2** - Zucchini producing faster than we can eat. Gave a bag to the neighbors.
Powdery mildew on lower leaves; removed them and improved airflow.

**Jul 30** - First ripe Sungold tomatoes! Sweet like candy.

## Lessons
- Start peppers indoors 10 weeks before transplant, not 6.
- Radishes bolt fast in heat; sow only in spring and fall.
- Compost tea seemed to help the lettuce.
