New engineer onboarding checklist - ClinicBook team

Day 1
  - Laptop setup: Xcode, Android Studio, Node 20, Yarn, Watchman
  - Get access: GitHub org, Jira project, Slack channels #clinicbook and #clinicbook-alerts
  - Complete the HIPAA privacy training module (required before touching production data)
  - Meet your onboarding buddy

Week 1
  - Build and run the app on a simulator and on a physical device
  - Read the architecture doc and the GraphQL schema
  - Pick up a "good first issue" ticket and open a pull request
  - Shadow the release manager during a release

Week 2
  - Join the on-call rotation as secondary (shadow only)
  - Present one thing you learned at the Friday demo

Accounts you will need
  - Apple developer account (ask the team lead)
  - Firebase console read access
  - TestFlight tester invite

Tips
  - Use the demo patient account "Jane Testpatient" for screenshots.
  - The staging clinics reset their schedules every night at midnight UTC.
