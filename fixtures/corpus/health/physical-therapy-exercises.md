# Physical therapy home program - right knee (patellofemoral pain)

Do once a day, 6 days a week. Stop if pain goes above 3 out of 10.

## Warm-up
- 10 minutes on the stationary bike, low resistance.

## Strengthening
1. **Straight leg raises** - lying on back, tighten the thigh, lift leg to the height of the other
   knee. 3 sets of 12.
2. **Clamshells** with a light resistance band above the knees - 3 x 15 each side.
   Strengthens the hip abductors, which keeps the knee from caving in.
3. **Wall sits** - 30 to 45 seconds, knees no further forward than the toes. 4 reps.
4. **Step-downs** from a 4 inch step, slow, watch that the knee tracks over the second toe. 3 x 10.
5. **Bridges** - 3 x 15, squeeze glutes at the top.

## Stretching
- Quad stretch standing, 30 seconds x 3.
- Hamstring stretch with a strap, 30 seconds x 3.
- Foam roll the outer thigh (IT band) 1-2 minutes.

## Progress
- Week 1: stairs hurt going down.
- Week 3: stairs fine, still sore after sitting through a long movie.
- Next appointment: reassess for adding single-leg squats and light jogging.
